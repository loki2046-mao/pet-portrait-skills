# 高奢大片摄影提示词

每张选一个主要情绪和造型重点。先选「新动物」或「具体个体编辑」，再附摄影语言；个体编辑的证据约束优先于构图和审美建议。

## 新动物创作

```text
Create ONE independent SQUARE 1:1 photorealistic luxury fashion photograph of a newly imagined [SPECIES / BREED]. This is a new fictional individual, not a reconstruction of a specific user's animal. Design unspecified individual traits consistently. Preserve species-appropriate anatomy, natural eye size, [FUR / FEATHERS / OTHER SURFACE] and a believable animal-tailored fit. Keep beaks, wings, paws and hooves appropriate to the species; no human torso, human hands or mammal lips on a bird.

The animal wears [ONE MAIN GARMENT OR A SMALL SPECIES-APPROPRIATE ACCESSORY] with [ZERO TO TWO REFINED ACCESSORIES]. Its mood is [QUIET CONFIDENCE / LANGUID EASE / PEACEFUL COMFORT / CONTEMPLATIVE MELANCHOLY / RESERVED ARTISTIC POISE]. Frame [HEAD / UPPER BODY / REQUESTED FRAMING] from [ANGLE], with [GAZE] and relaxed natural posture. Use believable portrait perspective and avatar-safe breathing room within a full square image, no circle drawn. The tactile setting is [SETTING].
```

## 用户照片中的具体个体

必须实际查看照片并先读 `photo-identity.md`，填写以下观察结果；不能用模板中的占位词代替真实检查。

```text
Edit the supplied IDENTITY photograph(s) of this particular animal. Image roles: [IDENTITY AND ANGLE IMAGE IDS]; [STYLE-ONLY IMAGE IDS, IF ANY]. Preserve the observed individual, not merely a representative of its species or breed.

Observed evidence: [SUPPORTED VIEW AND CROP]; [VISIBLE SILHOUETTE, FACIAL PROPORTIONS, EYES/NOSE/MUZZLE/BEAK/EARS]; [EXACT VISIBLE MARKING LOCATIONS AND BOUNDARIES, ASYMMETRIES AND DISTINCTIVE FEATURES]. Use image-relative location labels unless anatomical left/right is certain. Unknown or unverified: [OPPOSITE SIDE / HIDDEN EYE COLOR / BODY / PAWS / TAIL / MOUTH INTERIOR / OTHER SPECIFIC GAPS].

Keep [SUPPORTED CAMERA ANGLE, HEAD/BODY ORIENTATION AND VISIBLE EXTENT]. Do not horizontally flip, mirror markings, symmetrize the animal, fill unknown features from breed norms, or import anatomy from a style reference. Do not expose unknown regions or invent mouth/teeth details. Do not outpaint unobserved body parts to fit an outfit. Preserve the visible markings and geometry while changing only [APPROVED LIGHT / BACKGROUND / CLOTHING WITHIN OBSERVED GEOMETRY / FILM TREATMENT / SUPPORTED SMALL EXPRESSION CHANGE]. Keep [EXISTING WARDROBE OR OTHER FEATURES THE USER WANTS].

Express [MOOD] through supported gaze/posture, light, materials and composition. If only a single side or head is evidenced, retain that view and extent. Fit any scarf or collar to the observed neck without inventing a torso. Background extension for a square frame must not fabricate the animal's unseen anatomy. Identity evidence takes priority over a preferred side view, crop or series variation.
```

工具没有像素锁定就不要向用户承诺像素锁定。若请求超出证据且不能补图，先说明会沿用可见角度；若明确允许推测新视角，另行标注推测部分，不能混入上述忠实编辑承诺。

## 两种输入均可附加的摄影语言

```text
Light with [SOFT WINDOW SIDE LIGHT / SIDE-BACK LIGHT WITH GENTLE BOUNCE]. Shadows shape the face while retaining species-appropriate surface detail. Clothing shows authentic fibers, weight and natural folds; visible textures and subtle reflections remain believable.

An art-house cinema and luxury campaign finish: scanned high-speed 35mm motion-picture film aesthetics, perceptible fine organic grain, tactile photochemical density, separated smoky shadows, luminous midtones and softly compressed pearl-grey highlights. Gentle optical diffusion and trace halation only along truly bright edges. Keep the focal plane readable beneath grain. Restrained saturation, delicate slate / grey-green shadows, neutral-warm illuminated fur or feathers and ivory textiles. Retain natural individual color and visible marking boundaries.

No text, logos, watermark, collage or border. No CGI, plush toy, exaggerated eyes, human anatomy, plastic surfaces, smoothing, HDR sharpening, clutter, flat grey veil, white fog, heavy sepia, oversaturated teal-orange or gloomy underexposure.
```

## 仅作风格参照

```text
Use [IMAGE IDS] for STYLE ONLY: photographic light, color relationships, analog grain, tonal depth and textile realism. Do not copy the reference animal's identity, markings, anatomy, clothes or room. For a specific subject, all identity and permitted-view decisions come from its identity photographs.
```

## 新创作的镜头例子

- 蓝灰英短、燕麦羊绒和窄框墨镜，四分之三侧脸，窗影映在镜片上；自持、时髦。
- 惠比特、象牙高领和灰披肩，近正侧面望窗外；长鼻与颈部由侧逆光轻轻勾出。
- 兔子、颈部柔软燕麦针织小围巾，耳形自然，微侧安静出神；保持兔的头身比例。
- 小型鹦鹉、适合可见颈部的简洁织物造型，喙和翅保持真实结构；靠近窗光，羽色仍可辨认。

例子仅说明摄影语言，不要求用户照片换角度或新增身体。LOMO 日常抓拍由独立技能处理，不在本套提示词中切换风格。
